Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does verification take?

Verification time depends on how quickly the property owner responds — most are confirmed within a day.

Can I contact the owner before booking?

Yes — use the WhatsApp button on the property page to message the owner directly.

Where can I track my booking?

Visit the My Booking section after logging in to see live status updates.

What payment methods are accepted?

You can pay using any UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or by scanning the QR code shown at checkout.

Is the booking amount refundable if I cancel?

Refund policies vary by property owner. Check the cancellation terms on the property page, or message the owner on WhatsApp before booking.

What happens after I submit the booking form?

Your payment screenshot and details are sent to the owner for verification. Once approved, you'll get a confirmation and the booking status updates to "Confirmed" in My Booking.

Do I need to create an account to book a room?

Yes, a quick sign-up is required so you can track your booking status and receive updates from the owner.

What if my booking is rejected?

If an owner rejects a booking, your amount is refunded as per their policy and you're free to book another listing right away.
