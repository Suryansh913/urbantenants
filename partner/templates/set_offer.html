Why set a discount?
Most tenants compare 5–8 listings before deciding. A visible "% OFF" badge makes them click yours first.
Smart pricing strategy
Even a small 5–10% discount can cut vacancy from weeks to just a few days. The income lost on discount is far less than an empty room sitting idle for a month.
Higher visibility in search
Listings with an active offer appear above regular listings and get a highlighted badge on their card — making your room stand out instantly to browsing tenants.
Reduce costly vacancy time
Every empty day costs you money. Offers reduce average vacancy time from 18 days to just 6 days — fill rooms faster and keep your income steady throughout the year.
Attract serious tenants
Discounted rooms attract motivated tenants who are ready to move in quickly — fewer time-wasters, faster decisions, and better long-term occupancy rates.
Quick tips for best results
  • Start with 10–15% for the fastest bookings
  • Set a limited-time offer to create urgency for tenants
  • Combine with good photos for maximum impact
  • Even 5% is enough to get the offer badge and rank boost
  • Remove the offer once the room is filled
