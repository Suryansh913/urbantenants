The founding, in three moves

Room-hunting in Lucknow shouldn't feel like a gamble.

01
The Gap

Every year, thousands of students move to Lucknow for college and land in the same broken system — unverified listings, brokers who vanish after the deposit clears, and no real way to check a room before showing up with luggage in hand.

02
The Build

I built UrbanTenants to fix exactly that — alone. As a developer, I designed and coded the platform myself, from the listings engine to the booking flow, so renters get accurate details, real photos and videos, and a straightforward way to book a verified room without the guesswork.

03
What It Became

What started as a single Django project has grown into a full platform: partner onboarding for property owners, secure payments, and tools that make trust the default instead of the exception.

What drives the build

The principles behind every feature shipped on UrbanTenants.

V

Verified, not just listed

Every property goes through a partner onboarding process, so renters see real rooms, real amenities, and real pricing — no surprises at move-in.

S

Built for students first

Search, compare, and book from a phone in minutes. The platform is designed around how young renters actually search for a place to live.

L

Local, from day one

UrbanTenants started in Lucknow and stays close to the market it serves — understanding the neighbourhoods, the colleges, and the renters in them.

In his words

"I didn't want to build another listings site. I wanted to build the thing I wished existed when I was the one hunting for a room — something honest, fast, and built for the person moving in, not the broker collecting a fee."

Suryansh Shukla — Founder, UrbanTenants
