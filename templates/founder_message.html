Hi, I'm Suryansh Shukla, Founder of UrbanTenants.

UrbanTenants was created with one simple goal—to make finding rooms and PGs near Chandigarh University easier, faster, and more trustworthy.

We noticed that students spent countless hours searching through WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts, and unreliable listings. Property owners also struggled to connect with genuine tenants.

That's why UrbanTenants was built—to create a trusted platform where students and property owners can connect easily and confidently.

Our commitment is to provide verified listings, transparent information, and an excellent rental experience for everyone.

Thank you for trusting UrbanTenants and supporting our journey. We will continue improving the platform every single day.
