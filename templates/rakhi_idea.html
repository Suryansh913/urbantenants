The Concept

Ek sibling ghar se door hai. Iss baar Rakhi par, unka room hi unka ghar ban jaayega.

Participant ko imagine karna hai — unka sibling ek naye city me shift ho raha hai. Ab Gemini ki madad se unhe uske liye ek perfect living setup design karna hai: sahi budget, sahi location, safety, aur wo chhoti-chhoti cheezein jo kisi jagah ko "ghar" banati hain.

UrbanTenants yahan sirf ek platform nahi — story ka wo hissa hai jahan solution actually milta hai. Aur Gemini wo tool hai jo participant ki soch ko ek real, thoughtful design me badalta hai.

"Kisi ke liye ghar dhoondhna — asal me, unke liye pyaar dhoondhna hai."
